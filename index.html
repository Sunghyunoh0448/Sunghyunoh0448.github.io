Introduction

About me

Postdoctoral researcher specializing in hybrid quantum devices, superconducting circuits, Cu-Cu hybrid bonding, and multi-physics & multi-scale modeling and simulation. I am a member of the Quantum Device Group at the Korea Research Institute of Standards and Science (KRISS). My research combines the design, fabrication, measurement, and optimization of superconducting qubit system and micro&nano-electromechanical devices. I also have research experiences in multi-physics & multi-scale modeling and simulation of Cu-Cu hybrid bonding for advanced semiconductor packaging with finite element method. My current interests are hybrid quantum systems including photonic and phononic devices coupled with superconducting qubit. I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Sungkyunkwan University, where I investigated Cu–Cu bonding mechanisms for heterogeneous integration. My background spans advanced semiconductor packaging, thermal-mechanical analysis, microstructural evolution with phase-field analysis, and experimental validation.
